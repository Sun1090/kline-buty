# 04 · Trading Hours Overview: When Every Market Opens and Closes

> The market won't work overtime for you, but it will stage its riots while you sleep. **Trading hours are your risk's schedule**.
>
> All times in this article are **Beijing Time (UTC+8)**. North American sessions shift by 1 hour with daylight saving / standard time; always confirm actual times against the latest exchange announcements.

---

## 1. The Master Table First

| Market | Open | Close | Settlement | Holiday Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-shares | 09:30 | 15:00 (lunch break 11:30–13:00) | T+1 | Weekends + public holidays |
| HK stocks | 09:30 | 16:00 (lunch break 12:00–13:00) | T+0 / funds T+2 | Weekends + HK holidays |
| US stocks | 21:30 DST / 22:30 standard | Next day 04:00 / 05:00 | T+0 (PDT only) | Weekends + US holidays |
| Japanese stocks | 08:00 | 14:30 (lunch break 10:00–11:30) | T+0 | Weekends + Japanese holidays |
| Domestic commodity futures | 09:00 (night session from 21:00) | 15:00 (night session to next day 02:30) | T+0 | Weekends + long holidays |
| Stock index / treasury bond futures | 09:30 | 15:00 / 15:15 | T+0 | Closed in sync with the stock market |
| London gold & silver (spot, electronic market) | ~Mon 06:00 | ~Sat 05:00 | **<mark>Margin</mark>** T+0 | ~1 hour daily break |
| COMEX precious metals / crude futures | ~Mon 06:00 | ~Sat 05:00 | T+0 | 1-hour daily break |
| Forex | ~Mon 06:00 | ~Sat 05:00 | Margin T+0 | Weekend closure |
| Crypto | None | None | T+0 | **7×24×365, never closes** |

> One-line memory aid: **crypto is the most relentless (365 days a year), stocks are the most disciplined (4 hours a day), commodity futures work the hardest (day + night sessions bridging Europe and the US), gold and crude are the most accommodating (23 hours a day).**

## 2. Stock Market Trading Hours

### 2.1 A-Shares (Shanghai / Shenzhen)

| Session | Time |
|---|---|
| Call auction | 09:15–09:25 (no cancellation after 09:20); closing auction 14:57–15:00 |
| Continuous auction | 09:30–11:30, 13:00–15:00 |
| After-hours fixed-price trading | ChiNext / STAR Market 15:05–15:30 |

### 2.2 HK Stocks

- Pre-open auction 09:00–09:30, continuous auction 09:30–12:00 and 13:00–16:00, closing auction 16:00–16:10.
- Stock Connect eligibility and mainland holiday arrangements follow exchange announcements (the two regions' holidays don't align — "you're on holiday while HK stocks are trading" happens easily).

### 2.3 US Stocks (US Eastern ↔ Beijing Time)

| Session | US Eastern | Beijing (DST) | Beijing (standard time) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-market | 04:00–09:30 | 16:00–21:30 | 17:00–22:30 |
| Regular hours | 09:30–16:00 | 21:30–next day 04:00 | 22:30–next day 05:00 |
| After-hours | 16:00–20:00 | 04:00–08:00 | 05:00–09:00 |

- **Daylight saving (EDT)**: mid-March to early November; **standard time (EST)**: early November to mid-March the following year. On switchover days the session overnight "loses" or "gains" one hour.

## 3. Domestic Futures: A Three-Segment Schedule

### 3.1 Structure

```text
Day session: 09:00–10:15 (15-min break) → 10:30–11:30 → lunch → 13:30–15:00
Night session opens 21:00, closes in three tiers by product
```

- **Stock index futures (CFFEX)**: 09:30–11:30, 13:00–15:00, **no night session**, fully in sync with the stock market.
- **Treasury bond futures (CFFEX)**: 09:30–11:30, 13:00–15:15, no night session.

### 3.2 Night Session Closing Tiers (Beijing Time)

| Close | Representative products |
|---|---|
| 23:00 | Rebar, hot-rolled coil, rubber, fuel oil, bitumen, soybean meal, soybean oil, palm oil, corn, sugar, cotton, PTA, methanol, plastics, PP, iron ore, coke, coking coal, etc. |
| Next day 01:00 | Copper, aluminum, zinc, lead, nickel, tin, stainless steel, alumina |
| Next day 02:30 | **Gold, silver, crude oil (SC), low-sulphur fuel oil, international copper** |

- **No night session**: stock index futures, treasury bond futures, apples, red dates, eggs, japonica rice, fiberboard, plywood, etc.
- **Holidays**: domestic futures stay closed longer around New Year's Day / Spring Festival / National Day, while overseas markets (COMEX/NYMEX/LME) trade as usual — **the first open after a long holiday often gaps violently**, and holding positions overnight before the holiday is the most dangerous moment for futures beginners.

::: danger 💀 Post-Holiday Opening Gaps: The Deadliest Liquidation Hotspot
**The first open after a long holiday often gaps violently; carrying overnight positions into the holiday is the most dangerous moment for futures beginners.** Overseas markets trade as usual while you are away — price moves accumulated during the holiday are delivered all at once at the open.

> ⚠️ **Risk warning**: the night session exists for products that track European and US hours, and it is also the peak window for **<mark>liquidation</mark>** — traders who watch the screens all day tend to relax at night, precisely when markets are most active.

:::

## 4. Gold, Silver, Crude: The World's Main Trading Sessions

These three asset classes are global instruments whose hours span Asia, Europe, and the US. Understanding their schedule is a prerequisite for trading them.

### 4.1 London Gold & Silver (spot electronic market, e.g. XAUUSD/XAGUSD)

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hours | ~Mon 06:00 – ~Sat 05:00 (Beijing Time) |
| Daily break | ~05:00–06:00 (DST); the whole window shifts 1 hour in standard time |
| Nature | OTC market (LBMA system), dealer quotes, margin trading |
| Notes | The mainstream way to trade "spot gold"; **<mark>leverage</mark>** is high (commonly on the order of 1:100); **offshore broker platforms carry high risk — beware of fraudulent platforms** |

### 4.2 COMEX Precious Metals & NYMEX Energy (CME Group)

| Product | Code | Hours (Beijing Time) | Break |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold futures | GC | ~Mon 06:00 – ~Sat 05:00 | Daily ~05:00–06:00 |
| Silver futures | SI | Same as above | Same as above |
| WTI crude | CL | Same as above | Same as above |
| Natural gas | NG | Same as above | Same as above |

- Routine 1-hour maintenance break daily 17:00–18:00 (US Eastern).
- In standard time everything shifts 1 hour later (opens ~Mon 07:00 Beijing Time).

### 4.3 Brent Crude (ICE)

- The electronic market trades ~23 hours/day, roughly 08:00 – next day 06:00 Beijing Time, with a ~2-hour daily break.
- WTI (delivered at Cushing, USA) and Brent (delivered in the North Sea) are the world's two crude benchmarks; the **<mark>spread</mark>** between them reflects supply and demand on both sides of the Atlantic.

### 4.4 Gold / Crude Sessions Accessible from Mainland China

| Channel | Hours (Beijing Time) |
|---|---|
| Shanghai Gold Exchange gold/silver T+D | Day session 09:00–15:30, night session 20:00–next day 02:30 |
| INE crude futures SC | Day session 09:00–15:00, night session 21:00–next day 02:30 |
| SHFE gold/silver futures | Day session 09:00–15:00, night session 21:00–next day 02:30 |
| Bank paper gold | Most banks 24 hours or until around 04:00; see bank announcements |

### 4.5 Key Data Release Windows (Must-Watch for Gold / Crude Traders)

| Data | Release (Beijing Time) | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| US non-farm payrolls | First Friday of the month 20:30 (21:30 in standard time) | Violent moves in gold and USD |
| US CPI / PCE | Mid-month 20:30 (21:30 in standard time) | Rate expectations → gold, USD |
| Fed rate decisions | ~02:00 the day after the FOMC meeting (03:00 in standard time) | All markets |
| EIA crude inventories | Wednesdays 22:30 (23:30 in standard time) | Short-term crude direction |
| API crude inventories | ~04:30 the day before EIA | Leading indicator for crude |

## 5. Forex: Around the Clock, but Off on Weekends

- **Hours**: ~Mon 06:00 (Sydney open) → Tokyo → London → New York → ~Sat 05:00 (Beijing Time).
- **DST / standard time**: the whole window shifts by about 1 hour when Europe and the US switch.
- **Three peaks**: Tokyo open (~08:00), London open (15:00/16:00), New York open (21:30/22:30) — volatility concentrates in these overlapping windows.
- **Weekend**: closed Sat 05:00 – Mon 06:00; weekend positions carry "Sunday opening gap" risk.

## 7. What This Means in Practice

1. **Overnight risk is the most underestimated risk**: after domestic futures close at 15:00, overseas markets keep trading, and the next open directly reflects the overnight move — holding overnight = handing your fate to foreign markets.
2. **Pick "your" trading window**: office workers are best suited to A-shares, the first 2 hours of the night session, or crypto; full-time traders can exploit the Europe–US overlap.
3. **Cut position before major data**: the 30 minutes around NFP, CPI, and Fed decisions are peak "data sweep" windows that hit both longs and shorts.
4. **Reduce proactively before holidays**: mainland long holidays, US Thanksgiving/Christmas, and crypto weekend **<mark>liquidity</mark>** droughts all need advance planning.

::: warning ⚠️ Risk Warning
Trading hours are not "the time you are allowed to trade" — they are "the time your risk exposure exists". In time windows you don't understand, not opening a position is the best risk control there is.
:::
